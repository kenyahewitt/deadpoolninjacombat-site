What’s in the pack

A concise PDF rulebook plus a copy-paste example config. It covers the hard limits a trading bot should never turn off:

  • Per-trade cap and total-open cap
  • One open sleeve at a time
  • Stops, including a tighter early-tier stop, and flatten-on-slippage
  • Daily halt
  • KILL file (a local switch that blocks new buys)
  • Rugcheck before every buy
  • Liquidity floor, with an optional launch-curve path that still needs a fast exit
  • Bot-farm / clone skip, including factory-band lookalikes and brand-costume names
  • Breakeven lock
  • Exploit-coin skip for tokens named after a recent hack

The PDF also includes a take-profit and time-exit pair, a logging rule, and a one-page checklist. The numbers inside are example starter limits you can tighten for your own capital.
