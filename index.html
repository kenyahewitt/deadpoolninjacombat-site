Moves + gameplay

PC controls

A D move · Shift block · J punch · K kick or blade heavy · L weapon special · Q street dash · T tag · R rage · M moves.

Controller

D-pad or left stick moves. A light · Y heavy · B special · X block · RB tag · LB rage · L3 dash · Menu pauses.

Street utility

Build Street Heat with clean attacks. Grab floating neon drops for rage, armor, or score. Clear the active street contract for a mastery reward and a rage boost.

Weapon specials

Matrix Cap throws a ricochet shield. Rainbow Clan launches a prism shield return. Yellow Ninja spins a razor straw hat. Rasta Super fires heat vision. Ordi Nookie fires twin ordinal laser beams from his glasses.

Input buffering

Press the next attack before the current strike finishes. The game stores the input and releases it at the first legal frame so combo timing stays fast and responsive.

Tag + rage

Tag before a teammate is knocked out to protect their remaining health. At full Rage, activate an eight-second damage-pressure boost.

Mastery score

Damage, named combo finishers, wins, streaks, and knockouts award mastery XP. Build your best combo and raise your persistent combat level.

CPU rounds

Easy gives you a longer opening and lighter enemy damage. Medium is the balanced arcade fight. Very Difficult reacts faster, guards, chains combos, and awards the largest mastery multiplier.

New challengers

Allan is a fully human counter fighter with fast hands and a ground-smash special. Master Auren controls space with living-light saber waves. Ordi Nookie mixes pixel-powered punches, twin laser vision, and Inscription Overdrive.
