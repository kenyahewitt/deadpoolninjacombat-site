Privacy

Last updated August 30, 2026.

What this site does not collect

We do not ask for seed phrases, recovery phrases, or private keys. We do not operate a custodial wallet. We do not store your funds or NFTs on this server.

What may be stored on your device

The game and marketplace may save high scores, audio settings, player name, and verified fighter IDs in your browser localStorage so you can replay. You can clear that data in your browser. Wallet addresses are requested only if you click Connect wallet, and ownership checks are read-only against Robinhood Chain public RPC.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Online 1v1 uses PeerJS cloud signaling. NFT metadata may be fetched from public IPFS gateways and the Robinhood RPC. Linked markets (LaunchMyNFT, Ord Dropz, OpenSea, Lemon) have their own privacy practices. Hosting and analytics may be processed by Netlify as the site host.

Children

This entertainment site is not directed at children under 13. Speculative NFTs are not suitable for minors.
